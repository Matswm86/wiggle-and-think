# Animation / Video Brief — 20 Brain & Body Exercises (Ages 4-7)

Companion to `brain-activation-kids-4-7-2026-06-05.md`. One block per exercise for the animator/video creator.

**Conventions used below**
- **Shot:** camera angle + framing. (Side = profile; 3/4 = three-quarter front; Front = facing camera; Top = bird's-eye.)
- **Keyframes:** the 2-5 poses the motion passes through. A clean loop animates between these and returns to frame 1.
- **Loop:** where the clip seamlessly repeats (so it can run under any track length).
- **Tempo cue:** beats per movement, tied to the music band from the source doc.
- **On-screen:** minimal text/icon overlays a 4-7 yr-old (often pre-reader) can follow.

**Global style notes (apply to all 20)**
- One friendly character, full body always in frame, generous headroom + floor room (kids copy what they *see* edge-to-edge).
- High contrast character vs. plain background; the moving limb is the brightest/most saturated element.
- 2-3 second "get ready" hold on keyframe 1 before motion starts.
- Mirror logic: if you want kids to use their RIGHT, the character (facing them) uses its LEFT, OR shoot from behind. **Pick one rule and keep it across all 20.** Recommended: character faces camera and you color-code (right = blue limb, left = orange limb) instead of relying on left/right words.
- Soft "landing" squash-and-stretch on every jump; never a hard stick landing (models safe knees).
- Loop the motion 4-8× per clip; end on keyframe 1 for a clean stop.

## GROUP 1 — Animal Walks

### 1. Bear Crawl
- **Shot:** Side, tracking (camera dollies with the character) so it crawls across screen.
- **Keyframes:** (1) standing → (2) hands-down, hips up "table on all fours, knees off floor" → (3) right hand + right foot forward → (4) left hand + left foot forward.
- **Loop:** between KF3↔KF4 (the same-side step cycle). 4 step-cycles forward, then reverse the loop for "backward."
- **Tempo cue:** 1 limb-pair per beat, Band C (~100-120).
- **On-screen:** bear-ears on the character; ▶ arrow for direction.
- **Watch:** knees clearly OFF the floor; bottom up.

### 2. Crab Walk
- **Shot:** Side, tracking; character moves sideways across frame.
- **Keyframes:** (1) seated knees-up → (2) hands behind, hips lifted to flat "table" → (3) step right hand+foot → (4) follow with left hand+foot.
- **Loop:** KF3↔KF4.
- **Tempo cue:** 1 step per beat, Band C.
- **On-screen:** crab-claw hands icon.
- **Watch:** tummy stays UP and flat; fingers point toward feet.

### 3. Inchworm
- **Shot:** Side, locked (no tracking — it travels only slightly).
- **Keyframes:** (1) standing → (2) folded, hands on floor at feet → (3) hands walked out to plank → (4) feet walked up toward hands → back to (1) stand.
- **Loop:** full KF1→4→1, 1 worm per ~4-6 s.
- **Tempo cue:** slow, Band C low (~100). Hand-walk = 2-3 small beats, foot-walk = 2-3 small beats.
- **On-screen:** little wiggle/worm trail.
- **Watch:** straight legs in plank; back doesn't sag (cue a flat "tight tummy" line).

### 4. Flamingo Hop
- **Shot:** 3/4 front, locked.
- **Keyframes:** (1) stand, arms out as wings → (2) one foot lifted, knee bent → (3) mid-hop (standing foot off ground) → (4) soft landing on ball of foot.
- **Loop:** KF2↔KF4 for 5 hops, then mirror to the other foot.
- **Tempo cue:** 1 hop per beat, Band C.
- **On-screen:** flamingo color (pink); small "5" counter then a switch-feet swap icon.
- **Watch:** soft toe landings; wings stay out for balance.

## GROUP 2 — Two-Sides-Together (bilateral / midline)

### 5. Cross-Crawl March
- **Shot:** Front, locked (the crossing must read clearly).
- **Keyframes:** (1) stand tall → (2) RIGHT knee up + LEFT hand/elbow crossing to tap it → (3) back to stand → (4) LEFT knee up + RIGHT hand crossing.
- **Loop:** KF2↔KF4 (through a brief stand), ~20 taps.
- **Tempo cue:** 1 tap per beat, Band B march (~90-105).
- **On-screen:** color-code the crossing arm (it changes the body's midline — draw a faint vertical centre line the hand crosses).
- **Watch:** hand truly crosses the body's midline. (Caption framing: "both sides working together" — NOT brain/hemisphere language.)

### 6. Windmill Toe-Touch
- **Shot:** Front, locked.
- **Keyframes:** (1) feet wide, arms in a big T → (2) RIGHT hand swings down-across to LEFT foot, other arm up → (3) back to T → (4) LEFT hand to RIGHT foot.
- **Loop:** KF2↔KF4, 10 reps.
- **Tempo cue:** 1 reach per beat (or per 2 beats for the youngest), Band B.
- **On-screen:** windmill/pinwheel motif; centre line again.
- **Watch:** smooth, no bouncing; show a seated opposite-knee variant as a small inset.

### 7. Lazy-8 Air Tracing
- **Shot:** Front, chest-up framing (we need to see the thumb + eyes).
- **Keyframes:** trace path — (1) thumb at centre → (2) up-and-LEFT loop → (3) back through centre → (4) up-and-RIGHT loop → centre. Eyes/pupils follow the thumb; **head stays still.**
- **Loop:** the full ∞ path, 5-8 loops each hand; the glowing trail persists so kids see the shape.
- **Tempo cue:** slow + even, Band A-B; ~2 s per full 8.
- **On-screen:** draw the bright figure-8 trail; animate the eyes tracking it.
- **Watch:** keep the 8 BIG (shoulder-width); head doesn't turn. (Caption: "watch your thumb" — visual tracking, NOT Brain Gym.)

## GROUP 3 — Yoga & Balance

### 8. Tree Pose
- **Shot:** Front, locked, with a small wall "gaze dot" visible.
- **Keyframes:** (1) stand tall, eyes on the dot → (2) weight to one foot → (3) other sole to inner calf → (4) hands to heart or branches up. Hold.
- **Loop:** gentle 3-5 breath hold (subtle sway), then mirror sides.
- **Tempo cue:** Band A; breath-paced, no beat.
- **On-screen:** tree/leaf motif; a pulsing "gaze dot." Foot placement highlighted ABOVE/BELOW knee with a small ✓ (never on the knee — show a ✗ on the knee).
- **Watch:** the foot-not-on-knee rule must be unmistakable.

### 9. Airplane Pose
- **Shot:** Side, locked (the level body line reads best in profile).
- **Keyframes:** (1) stand, arms as wings → (2) weight to one foot → (3) tip forward, lift back leg → (4) arms-torso-leg form one level line. Hold 3-5 s.
- **Loop:** hold with tiny wobble, then mirror.
- **Tempo cue:** Band A.
- **On-screen:** airplane motif; a faint horizontal guide line the body matches.
- **Watch:** slow entry, no leg-swing; show a chair-support inset for beginners.

### 10. Cat-Cow
- **Shot:** Side, locked.
- **Keyframes:** (1) hands-knees table → (2) COW: belly drops, head up, back bowed (breathe in) → (3) CAT: back rounds up, chin tucks (breathe out).
- **Loop:** KF2↔KF3, 5 rounds.
- **Tempo cue:** Band A, breath-paced (~3-4 s each way).
- **On-screen:** cat ↔ cow icon swaps with the in/out breath arrows.
- **Watch:** wrists under shoulders.

### 11. Downward Dog
- **Shot:** Side, locked.
- **Keyframes:** (1) hands-knees → (2) toes tuck, hips lift to inverted-V → (3) heels reach down, head hangs → (4) "tail wag" hips side to side.
- **Loop:** small KF3↔KF4 wag during a 3-5 breath hold.
- **Tempo cue:** Band A.
- **On-screen:** dog motif; upside-down-V guide.
- **Watch:** soft elbows; heels needn't touch.

## GROUP 4 — Listen-and-Move Games

### 12. Freeze Dance
- **Shot:** Front, locked, roomy frame.
- **Keyframes:** DANCE = a 2-4 pose free-dance cycle (bounce, arms up, twist, wiggle); FREEZE = one held statue pose. Then a "big breath" before unfreeze.
- **Loop:** loop the dance cycle; trigger a hard FREEZE on the music stop, hold ~2-3 s, resume.
- **Tempo cue:** Band D (120-145) with **hard cuts** (animate the stop in sync with the audio cut, screen flashes "FREEZE").
- **On-screen:** big ❄️ FREEZE card on stop; "safe space, arms apart" reminder at start.
- **Watch:** the stop must be instant (this IS the self-control skill). Model a clean statue, no collisions.

### 13. Simon Says — Coordination Edition
- **Shot:** Front, locked; a "Simon" leader figure + the move demo.
- **Keyframes:** per command: (1) leader speaks → (2) decision beat → (3a) "Simon says" → do the move / (3b) no "Simon says" → stay still. Cycle through midline + balance moves (right-hand-to-left-knee, one-leg stand, star).
- **Loop:** episodic, not a tight loop — string 4-6 command mini-clips.
- **Tempo cue:** Band B low or none; verbal cues must be audible.
- **On-screen:** speech bubble shows ✅ "Simon says…" vs ⛔ (no Simon) to teach the inhibition rule visually.
- **Watch:** clearly show the "don't move" correct response on the trick commands.

## GROUP 5 — Rhythm & Body Percussion

### 14. Clap Call-and-Response
- **Shot:** Front, chest-up (hands are the star).
- **Keyframes:** leader claps pattern → group echoes. Animate hands meeting on each clap with a small impact pop.
- **Loop:** 4-beat call, 4-beat echo; grow patterns (clap/thigh-pat/stomp/snap).
- **Tempo cue:** Band E (80-116), VERY clear pulse; flash a beat-dot on each hit.
- **On-screen:** "your turn" / "my turn" swap; beat-dots row that lights up.
- **Watch:** beat-dots make the rhythm visible for non-readers; offer knee-pat instead of stomp (small icon).

### 15. Stomp-Clap-Pat Body Drum
- **Shot:** Front, full body (feet + hands both visible).
- **Keyframes:** STOMP (foot down) → STOMP → CLAP (hands) → PAT (thighs). Each with an impact pop on the limb used.
- **Loop:** the 4-hit pattern ×4, then "speed up / slow down / eyes closed" variants.
- **Tempo cue:** Band E; light up the body part on each hit.
- **On-screen:** a 4-cell pattern strip (foot, foot, hands, thighs) that advances with the beat.
- **Watch:** show the knee-pat-instead-of-stomp inset for hard floors.

## GROUP 6 — Jumps & Aerobic

### 16. Kangaroo / Rocket Jumps
- **Shot:** Side or 3/4 front, locked.
- **Keyframes:** (1) paws at chest, slight squat → (2) explosive push-off → (3) peak height, body long → (4) **soft** bent-knee landing (squash).
- **Loop:** KF1→4→1, 8-10 jumps.
- **Tempo cue:** Band D; 1 jump per ~1-2 beats.
- **On-screen:** kangaroo/rocket motif; little "soft landing" cushion poof on KF4.
- **Watch:** exaggerate the soft, bent-knee landing every single time.

### 17. Star Jumps
- **Shot:** Front, locked (the star shape needs front view).
- **Keyframes:** (1) feet together, soft knees → (2) push-off → (3) **star/X in the air**, arms+legs out → (4) soft landing, feet together.
- **Loop:** KF1→4→1, 8-12 jumps.
- **Tempo cue:** Band D.
- **On-screen:** star burst on KF3.
- **Watch:** soft landings; mark as "after you can do jumping jacks."

### 18. Jumping Jacks
- **Shot:** Front, locked.
- **Keyframes:** (1) feet together, arms down → (2) mid-jump → (3) feet apart + arms clapped overhead.
- **Loop:** KF1↔KF3, 10-20.
- **Tempo cue:** Band D low (~120).
- **On-screen:** ⚠️ include the 2-step teaching insets: (A) feet-only apart/together, (B) arms-only up/down, then (C) combined — for 4-year-olds.
- **Watch:** soft landings; the broken-down version is essential for the youngest.

## GROUP 7 — Dynamic Balance

### 19. Tightrope Walk
- **Shot:** Side, tracking along the line; a clear floor LINE drawn.
- **Keyframes:** (1) arms out, one foot on line → (2) heel-to-toe step → (3) next heel-to-toe step. Eyes forward.
- **Loop:** KF2↔KF3 along the line; then a backward variant + beanbag-on-head variant inset.
- **Tempo cue:** Band B, slow + steady.
- **On-screen:** the tape line glows; eyes-forward reminder (✗ on looking-down).
- **Watch:** heel touches toe; arms stay out.

## GROUP 8 — Calm-Down / Breathing

### 20. Starfish Breathing (+ alternates)
- **Shot:** Front, chest-up / hand close-up.
- **Keyframes (Starfish):** tracing finger goes UP outside of thumb (breathe IN) → DOWN inside (breathe OUT), across all 5 fingers. Slow glowing trail along the hand outline.
- **Alternate A — Balloon Belly:** Side view; belly inflates on IN (4 ct), deflates on OUT (4 ct); one hand on belly rises/falls.
- **Alternate B — Rainbow Breath:** Front; arms sweep up a rainbow arc on IN, down on OUT (the arc draws/erases).
- **Loop:** 5 slow breaths; pure breath pace, NO beat.
- **Tempo cue:** Band A (55-75) ambient bed.
- **On-screen:** an expanding/contracting circle or the breath arc as the visual breath-pacer; "breathe in" / "breathe out" with arrows.
- **Watch:** slow and gentle; this is the session closer — wind the energy DOWN.

## Production checklist (hand to the animator)

- [ ] Pick ONE mirror convention (recommend: character faces camera + color-coded limbs) and apply to all 20.
- [ ] Every clip: 2-3 s ready-hold → looping motion (4-8×) → clean stop on frame 1.
- [ ] Soft squash landing on all jumps (1, 3 frog/4 flamingo, 16, 17, 18).
- [ ] Beat-dot / pattern-strip overlays on rhythm clips (14, 15) for non-readers.
- [ ] Safety insets where flagged: knee-pat alt (14,15), 2-step jumping jack (18), chair support (9), seated variant (6), foot-placement ✓/✗ (8).
- [ ] Loop points are seamless so any music-track length works.
- [ ] Match clip motion tempo to the exercise's music band (A-E) from the source doc.
