# Add-on Brief — Finger & Hand Coordination Moves ("Clever Hands")

Companion to the original `brain-activation-kids-4-7-2026-06-05.md` + `...ANIMATION-BRIEF...`.

**Primary source (better than the Yoga Guppy video):** ETNIMU — *"Exercises to Activate the Brain"*,
Society for Memory Disorders Expertise in Finland (Suomen muistiasiantuntijat ry), Publication 21/2019,
ISBN 978-952-7227-29-9. https://www.muistiasiantuntijat.fi/etnimu — 22 coordination & concentration
exercises. (Originally for elderly groups, seated; they adapt cleanly to kids as finger games.)

## FRAMING (keep the site's honest identity)
ETNIMU's own framing is defensible and matches our site: *"coordination and concentration exercises that
support brain health by improving interaction between the brain and muscles… new skills open new
connections… training of concentration."* That is **not** the Brain-Gym left/right-hemisphere neuromyth.
On the site, label these as **fine-motor / finger-dexterity / crossing-the-middle / concentration**
(visuomotor skill = strongest predictor of maths & reading readiness). Never "activates the brain hemispheres."

## AGE NOTE (4–7)
Several of these are genuinely hard, even for adults, that is the point (ETNIMU: *"It doesn't matter if you
don't succeed right away, you are allowed to have a good laugh at yourself."*). Every move below has an
**Easy start** for 4-year-olds and a **Make it tricky** step for 6–7s, so the same card grows with the child.

## 1. Finger Tips  (thumb-to-finger opposition — ETNIMU "Finger curl")
- **Kid line:** "Touch your thumb to each finger — pointer, tall one, ring, pinky!"
- **Do it:** Both hands at once: thumb taps the tip of the index, then middle, then ring, then little finger,
  then back down. Say the count out loud.
- **Easy start:** one hand only, thumb to index and back. **Make it tricky:** one hand goes thumb→pinky while
  the OTHER hand goes pinky→thumb at the same time (the two hands do opposite orders).
- **Builds:** Fine-motor finger control + hand-eye skill (strongest link to maths & reading); counting = the
  thinking layer. **Evidence:** MODERATE-STRONG.
- **Music:** Band A–B (~75–90). **Safety:** gentle taps, no strain.
- **Animation:** Front, hands big. KF: thumb meets index → middle → ring → little (4 clear touch poses, small
  "tap" pop each). Loop forward then reverse. On-screen: a 1-2-3-4 dot row lights per tap. Watch: the meeting
  fingertips touch cleanly; the other fingers stay extended.

## 2. Open & Close Race  (ETNIMU "Closing and opening the fingers")
- **Kid line:** "One hand opens while the other hand closes, at the same time!"
- **Do it:** Both hands start as gentle fists, palms up. Open the LEFT hand one finger at a time starting from
  the thumb; at the SAME time close the RIGHT hand one finger at a time starting from the little finger. Then
  swap which hand opens and which closes.
- **Easy start:** just open one hand finger-by-finger (thumb first), other hand rests.
  **Make it tricky:** keep the steady open-one/close-one going back and forth without stopping.
- **Builds:** Each hand doing the opposite thing (bilateral independence), finger sequencing, concentration.
  **Evidence:** MODERATE.
- **Music:** Band A–B (slow). **Safety:** gentle, never force the stretch.
- **Animation:** Front, hands big. KF: L-hand fist→thumb out→+index→+middle→+ring→+pinky = open, while R-hand
  open→pinky in→+ring→+middle→+index→+thumb = fist (mirrored counter-motion). Loop, then reverse roles.
  On-screen: a small ▲ over the opening hand, ▼ over the closing hand. Watch: the two hands are always in
  opposite states.

## 3. Finger Twirls  (ETNIMU "Rotation of fingers" / "A ball formed by hands")
- **Kid line:** "Make a ball with your hands and twirl your fingers around each other!"
- **Do it:** Cup both hands into a hollow "ball," fingertips of each hand touching their twin. Twirl the two
  thumbs around each other (like thumb-war), then change direction. Then twirl just the index fingers, then
  the middle, ring, and little fingers, each pair circling without the pairs touching.
- **Easy start:** just twirl the thumbs (thumb war), both directions. **Make it tricky:** index fingers only,
  keeping every other finger still and touching.
- **Builds:** Isolating one finger pair, fine control, focus. **Evidence:** MODERATE.
- **Music:** Band A. **Safety:** slow, gentle.
- **Animation:** Front, hands cupped close-up. KF: the named finger pair circles (thumbs, then index, …),
  one pair moving while the rest stay touching. Loop per finger, with a direction-flip. On-screen: a small ⟳/⟲
  by the active pair. Watch: only the named pair moves.

## 4. Tap Pairs  (ETNIMU "Fingers on a table")
- **Kid line:** "Pop two fingers up at a time, as quick as you can!"
- **Do it:** Rest hands on a table (or thighs). Lift these finger pairs in order, as quick as you can:
  index + middle → middle + ring + little → index + little → repeat.
- **Easy start:** just index + middle, up and down. **Make it tricky:** both hands at the same time.
- **Builds:** Finger isolation, quick sequencing, rhythm. **Evidence:** MODERATE.
- **Music:** Band B–E (clear pulse, ~90). **Safety:** light taps, soft surface.
- **Animation:** Front, hands flat on a line (table edge). KF: the named fingers lift then lower (3 patterns),
  little "tap" pop on each lift. Loop the 3-pattern cycle. On-screen: highlight which fingers lift. Watch:
  only the named fingers lift; the rest stay down.

## 5. Thumbs In, Thumbs Out  (ETNIMU "Turning fists")
- **Kid line:** "Fists out front, thumbs pop OUT, then tuck IN!"
- **Do it:** Hold both fists out in front, palms facing opposite ways. Turn the fists one way and pop the
  thumbs OUT; turn them the other way and tuck the thumbs IN. Keep turning in and out.
- **Easy start:** both hands the same. **Make it tricky:** one thumb out while the other is in, then swap.
- **Builds:** Thumb control, both-hands timing, listening (if a grown-up calls "in/out"). **Evidence:** MODERATE.
- **Music:** Band B. **Safety:** gentle.
- **Animation:** Front, two fists. KF: (1) fists turned, thumbs out → (2) fists turned, thumbs tucked. Loop.
  On-screen: "in / out" pop text. Watch: thumbs clearly pop out and tuck.

## 6. Fist & Flat Swap  (ETNIMU "Swapping of lifted arms")
- **Kid line:** "One hand makes a fist, one hand goes flat, now swap!"
- **Do it:** Right arm out to the side as a FIST; left hand FLAT on your chest. Swap: left arm out as a fist,
  right hand flat on the chest. The hand out to the side is always a fist.
- **Easy start:** just swap the hands slowly. **Make it tricky:** tap your heels on the floor at the same time.
- **Builds:** Two hands doing different jobs, crossing toward the middle, timing. **Evidence:** MODERATE.
- **Music:** Band B (~90). **Safety:** slow first.
- **Animation:** Front, full upper body. KF: (1) R-arm out fist + L-hand flat on chest → (2) both mid-swap →
  (3) L-arm out fist + R-hand flat. Loop. Color-code arms. Watch: the side arm is a fist, the chest hand is flat.

## 7. Magic Numbers  (ETNIMU "Numbers by hands")
- **Kid line:** "Draw a number in the air with one hand, and backwards with the other!"
- **Do it:** Both arms stretched out, pointer fingers up. With the RIGHT hand draw a big number in the air
  (1, 2, 3…); with the LEFT hand draw the SAME number as a mirror image at the same time.
- **Easy start:** both hands draw a big circle together. **Make it tricky:** count up to 10, mirrored each time.
- **Builds:** Both sides together (bilateral), pre-writing, number practice. **Evidence:** MODERATE (writing readiness).
- **Music:** Band A–B. **Safety:** big slow shapes; works seated.
- **Animation:** Front, both arms/hands, full-body rig. KF: both index fingers trace a mirrored number with a
  glowing symmetric trail across a centre line. Loop per number. On-screen: the number ghosted behind the
  trails. Watch: the two trails mirror exactly.

## 8. Nose & Ear Switch  (ETNIMU "Touching the nose and ear")
- **Kid line:** "Hand over your head to the other ear, other hand on your nose, switch!"
- **Do it:** Bring your RIGHT hand over the top of your head to your LEFT ear, and your LEFT hand to your nose.
  Stretch both hands forward. Then bring the LEFT hand over the top to the RIGHT ear and the RIGHT hand to the
  nose. (A clap in the middle is a fun extra.)
- **Easy start:** simple version, hand to opposite ear at shoulder height, other hand on nose, switch.
  **Make it tricky:** do it while stepping forward and back.
- **Builds:** Crossing the middle, hand-eye-body coordination, attention. **Evidence:** MODERATE.
- **Music:** Band B (~85–95). **Safety:** gentle on the nose, slow.
- **Animation:** Front, chest/head-up. KF: (1) R-hand over head to L-ear + L-hand on nose → (2) both stretch
  forward (or clap) → (3) L-hand over head to R-ear + R-hand on nose. Loop. On-screen: faint centre line.
  Watch: the ear hand goes up and over the head, clearly crossing to the OTHER side.
